Place your optional local images in this folder (`public/assets/`):
1. Candidate Publicity Banner Image:
   - Example: Save your banner as `public/assets/candidate-banner.jpg`
   - Then open `src/config/siteConfig.js` and set:
     export const CANDIDATE_BANNER_URL = "/assets/candidate-banner.jpg";

2. Optional Website Background Image:
   - Example: Save your background image as `public/assets/background.jpg`
   - Then open `src/config/siteConfig.js` and set:
     export const BACKGROUND_IMAGE_URL = "/assets/background.jpg";
